## 1. Purpose

Establish a standardized process for the identification, documentation, classification, and first-response handling of customer complaints about Sweet July Skin products, so that every complaint is captured consistently and routed to the right next step.

## 2. Scope

Applies to any end-customer quality signal about a Sweet July Skin product, however it arrives — direct email, retail-partner ping, marketplace review, phone call, or the Asana intake form. Does not cover adverse events meeting SKN-OPS-002 criteria or recall triggers meeting SKN-OPS-003 criteria — those escalate out of this procedure and into their own.

## 3. Definitions

- **Complaint** — any customer-reported dissatisfaction with a product's performance, packaging, application, or sensory experience that does not meet the SAE or recall definitions.
- **First response corrective action** — the immediate action taken in response to a complaint (e.g., replace, refund, no return), as distinct from any longer-run corrective/preventive action opened as a CAPA.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Reporter** — any employee or system that captures a customer complaint at first contact.
- **Operator** — reviews intake, classifies the complaint, recommends and confirms first-response action.
- **Customer Service** — executes the first-response action once confirmed.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Intake

Every complaint is logged with, at minimum: the customer signal itself, SKU, product batch code if present, sales channel, customer name, order/invoice number, and a free-text description. If a batch code is reported, it is cross-checked against the production and fulfillment record before being trusted, rather than taken at face value — a customer-reported lot number is sometimes misread.

### 5.2 Classification and First Response

Each complaint is classified into one of a defined set of complaint types, and a first-response corrective action is recommended based on that type. Classification and first-response action are recommendations subject to confirmation before anything is executed — neither commits silently.

### 5.3 Trend Monitoring

Complaints are read on a recurring basis across three lenses — by SKU, by complaint type, by batch — to catch a pattern before it becomes a bigger problem. A pattern that crosses a defined threshold (e.g., repeated complaints on one batch, or repeated same-type complaints on one SKU within a set window) escalates into the CAPA process (SKN-OPS-001).

### 5.4 Escalation

A complaint that reads as a possible adverse event escalates immediately to the SAE procedure (SKN-OPS-002). A complaint pattern, or a single severe event, that reads as a possible recall trigger surfaces the question to the recall procedure (SKN-OPS-003) — it does not start a recall on its own.

## 6. Documentation

Every complaint and its resolution is retained as a record, with first-response action, evidence, and any downstream escalation (CAPA, SAE, recall) cross-referenced.

## 7. Review and Approval

This SOP is reviewed annually and updated as necessary. All revisions are approved by the QA Manager.
