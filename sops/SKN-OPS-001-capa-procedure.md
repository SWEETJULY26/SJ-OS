# Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) Procedure

> **Overdue for annual review** as of this migration (due 2026-06-30). Carried forward from the quality-manager SOP catalog rather than silently reset — the review should happen before this note is removed.

## 1. Purpose

Establish a systematic approach for identifying, documenting, analyzing, and implementing corrective and preventive actions to address non-conformances and potential non-conformances within Sweet July Beauty, LLC.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all departments and personnel involved in the identification, documentation, investigation, and resolution of non-conformances and potential non-conformances at Sweet July Beauty, LLC.

## 3. Definitions

- **Corrective Action** — action taken to eliminate the cause of a detected non-conformance or other undesirable situation.
- **Preventive Action** — action taken to eliminate the cause of a potential non-conformance or other undesirable potential situation.
- **Non-Conformance** — a deviation from a standard or specification that may affect product quality, safety, or regulatory compliance.

## 4. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Quality Assurance (QA) | Overall responsibility for the CAPA system; compliance with this SOP; maintaining CAPA records |
| Department Managers | Ensure non-conformances within their department are reported, investigated, and resolved |
| All Employees | Report non-conformances and potential non-conformances to their supervisors or QA |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Identification and Documentation

Any employee identifying a non-conformance or potential non-conformance reports it immediately to their supervisor or QA designee, and completes a Non-Conformance Report (NCR) covering the details of the non-conformance, date, and responsible person. The structured intake fields and severity bands used to fill out an NCR are defined in SKN-OPS-005 (Non-Conformance Report Procedure), which exists specifically to close the gap this section leaves open.

### 5.2 Investigation

**Initial Assessment.** QA reviews the NCR form to determine whether a CAPA is required. If so, a CAPA number is assigned and a CAPA file opened. SKN-OPS-005 §4 supplies the review checklist and the three decision branches (convert to CAPA / close — no CAPA / hold for more information).

**Root Cause Analysis.** A CAPA investigation team forms, including relevant personnel, and conducts a root cause analysis using an appropriate tool (e.g., Fishbone Diagram, 5 Whys — see Appendix C). Default: 5 Whys for single-event causes, Fishbone for systemic or multi-factor causes; QA Lead can override.

### 5.3 Action Plan

**Corrective Action Plan** — addresses the root cause of the non-conformance: actions, responsibilities, and timelines.

**Preventive Action Plan** — addresses potential non-conformances the investigation surfaces: actions, responsibilities, and timelines.

Both plans use the CAPA Investigation Template (Appendix B). Each action item carries an action, an owner (role and person), a date-bounded timeline, and a description of the evidence required to mark it complete. A preventive action that implies a SOP change routes a SOP-revision request per §7.

### 5.4 Implementation

Corrective and preventive actions are implemented per the defined plan. Progress and completion are documented in the CAPA file as they happen, with evidence attached.

### 5.5 Verification and Effectiveness

**Verification** — QA confirms all actions were completed as planned.

**Effectiveness Review** — a separate step confirming the corrective action actually eliminated the root cause, not just that the actions happened. Findings are documented in the CAPA file. If effectiveness fails, the CAPA reopens at Investigation (§5.2) with the failure as new evidence.

Effectiveness review windows, by severity (bands defined in SKN-OPS-005 §3.3): Critical 90 days, Major 60 days, Minor 30 days.

### 5.6 Documentation and Records

A CAPA file is maintained for each CAPA: NCR form, investigation reports, action plans, verification records, effectiveness reviews. Retention floor: 3 years post-batch-expiration (ISO 22716 cosmetic GMP).

## 6. Training

All employees involved in the CAPA process receive appropriate training on this SOP. Training records are documented.

## 7. Review and Approval

This SOP is reviewed annually and updated as necessary. All revisions are approved by the QA Manager.
