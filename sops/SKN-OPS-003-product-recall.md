# Product Recall Procedure

> **Overdue for annual review** as of this migration (due 2026-06-30).

## 1. Purpose

Establish a process for recalling skincare products in case of a quality issue or safety concern.

## 2. Scope

All employees responsible for the recall of skincare products manufactured by Sweet July Beauty, LLC, including the Quality Control Team, Production Team, and Customer Service Team.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Quality Control Team** — identifies the issue requiring a recall, determines scope, identifies affected batches.
- **Production Team** — identifies root cause, takes corrective and preventive actions to prevent recurrence.
- **Customer Service Team** — notifies customers, coordinates the return of affected products.
- **Management Team** — oversees the recall process, reviews corrective and preventive actions, ensures appropriate actions are taken.

## 4. Definitions

- **Recall** — the removal of affected product from distribution and/or customers in response to a confirmed quality issue or safety concern, initiated explicitly and never inferred from a pattern alone.

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Identify the Issue

The Quality Control Team identifies the issue requiring recall — a quality issue or safety concern. A recall is only ever initiated on an explicit decision naming the affected SKU or batch; a pattern surfaced elsewhere (complaint trend, SAE investigation) raises the question, it does not start the recall on its own.

### 5.2 Scope of Recall

The Quality Control Team determines scope: affected batches, distribution channels, and customer information. Batch context, unit counts, and distribution split by channel are pulled from the PLM system of record.

### 5.3 Notification

The Customer Service Team notifies customers via email, phone, or letter depending on severity. Notification includes return instructions and refund or replacement options. Retail partner and external-authority notifications are separate cross-functional asks — Operations owns retail partner notice, Regulatory owns notice to external authorities.

### 5.4 Return and Disposal of Affected Products

The Customer Service Team coordinates return and disposal of affected product per applicable laws and regulations.

### 5.5 Corrective and Preventive Actions

The Production Team identifies root cause and takes corrective and preventive actions to prevent recurrence, with actions and timelines documented. This step feeds the CAPA process (SKN-OPS-001).

### 5.6 Follow-up

The Customer Service Team follows up with customers to confirm products were returned and issues resolved.

## 6. Documentation

All recall-related documents — customer notification, return and disposal records, corrective and preventive actions — are documented and maintained in a recall log.

## 7. Training

All employees in the recall process receive training on this SOP and understand their roles.

## 8. Review

This SOP is reviewed and updated annually or as needed.

## 9. Communication

This SOP is communicated to all employees in the recall process. Updates are communicated promptly.

## 10. Record Retention

All recall records are retained for a minimum of 5 years, or per company policy if longer.

## 11. Operating principles (non-negotiable)

- **Kickoff is explicit, never inferred.** A pattern in trend analysis or SAE escalation that suggests a recall surfaces the question — it does not start the recall.
- **Step-by-step approval.** Each step in §5 is its own sign-off gate; none proceeds on a blanket approval.
- **Customer communications are drafted, never auto-sent.**
- **A cross-functional ask list with named owners (Customer Service, Operations, Regulatory, Production) is mandatory output of every recall** — no single function can drive the whole recall alone.
